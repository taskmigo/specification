# 10. Traceability and Unresolved Issues

## 10.1 Traceability Approach

Each normative requirement has a stable identifier, an observable verification objective, and a traceability statement. The requirement-to-verification index is maintained in the [verification and conformance matrix](09-verification-validation-and-acceptance.md#91-verification-and-conformance-matrix). Scope and product-context relationships are linked from each requirement to the relevant [introduction](01-introduction.md), [overall description](02-overall-description.md), interface, behavior, data, quality, or constraint section.

## 10.2 Unresolved Issues

No unresolved conflict is recorded in this SRS. Deferred client filtering, relationship predicates, and additional operators are recorded as non-normative future extensions in [Appendix B](11-appendices.md#111-future-extensions).
